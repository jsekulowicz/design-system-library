Add `ds-step-list`, a numbered progress trail for stepped forms. Renders steps as done, current or upcoming, emits `ds-step-select` for any step the host has not disabled, and condenses to the current step plus a segmented rail in a container narrower than the `md` breakpoint, or at any width with `compact`.
