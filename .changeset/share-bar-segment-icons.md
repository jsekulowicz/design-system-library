`ds-share-bar` data take an optional `icon`, drawn on a square tile in its share's color and centered on the share, so it stays legible on a thin bar. The tile is the `segment-icon` part, sized by `--ds-share-bar-icon-size` and `--ds-share-bar-icon-tile-size`, rounded by `--ds-share-bar-icon-radius` and colored by `--ds-share-bar-icon-color`.
