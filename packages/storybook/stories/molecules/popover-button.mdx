# PopoverButton

PopoverButton opens a neutral, anchored panel from a button. Use it for compact interactive content such as preferences, filters, or notifications that does not follow menu semantics.

## Anatomy

PopoverButton provides a default button or accepts a custom trigger through the `trigger` slot. The default slot contains the panel content. The component manages positioning, disclosure state, outside-click dismissal, Escape dismissal, and focus return while leaving the panel's content and semantics to the consumer.

  <ds-tab-panel value="guidelines">
    ### Do

    - Use PopoverButton for rich or mixed interactive content that is not a menu.
    - Give the trigger a meaningful visible label or accessible name.
    - Give panel content suitable semantics and an accessible name where needed.
    - Choose a placement that keeps the panel close to its trigger and within the viewport.
    - Keep the content concise and suitable for a non-modal interaction.

    ### Don't

    - Don't use PopoverButton for a list of actions; use MenuButton instead.
    - Don't use it for complex or modal tasks that require focus trapping; use Dialog instead.
    - Don't manually manage `aria-expanded` or `aria-controls` on a custom trigger.
    - Don't rely on the popover to provide roles or labels for its slotted content.

    ### Accessibility

    The trigger exposes disclosure state through `aria-expanded` and associates itself with the open panel through `aria-controls`. Escape closes the panel and returns focus to the trigger. Clicking outside also closes it. Because the component does not impose a role or focus model on the panel, consumers must provide semantic structure, accessible names, and a logical tab order for slotted content.
